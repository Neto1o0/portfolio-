Origins

I was born in Santiago, the bustling capital of Chile, but my family's roots lie deeply in rural living. Growing up between these two contrasting environments taught me to appreciate the best of both worlds. I learned to value the boundless freedom, independence, and tranquility of a rural lifestyle, while simultaneously embracing the vibrant energy and diverse social opportunities that a massive city offers. This duality fueled my lifelong dream of traveling the globe to experience new cultures—a dream I proudly achieved later in my life.

Team work

Back in fifth grade, I joined my school's volleyball team and immediately fell in love with the sport. As my athletic skills evolved, new doors opened to compete in increasingly prestigious teams, inherently sharpening my teamwork capabilities. One of my proudest achievements was playing for Chile's National Team. Competing at that elite level scaled my skills exponentially—teaching me discipline, high-stakes collaboration, and leadership—many of which I seamlessly apply to my professional life today.

Problem solving

My early years in college taught me the true essence of being an engineer. I became deeply fascinated by projects that solve everyday, real-world problems in innovative ways I could never have imagined. Through these challenges, my vision completely transformed; I now see engineering not just as math, but as a vital pillar that humanity simply could not live without. This profound realization inspires my relentless drive to build meaningful projects that make a genuine difference in society.

Multicultural awareness

In 2025, I embarked on an international exchange program to the Netherlands through my university—an adventure that became the most defining experience of my life. I was immersed not just in one new culture, but a rich melting pot of global perspectives. Collaborating closely on a high-impact project with brilliant minds from diverse nationalities pushed my adaptability to the absolute limit. I knew that living in such an advanced nation would accelerate my personal growth, so I made the most of every second. Our project was a massive success, and I proudly maintain lasting connections with both our clients and my remarkable teammates.

Versatility

One thing I am deeply proud of is my relentless drive to specialize in every pursuit I dedicate myself to—whether it be music, computer science, high-level sports, or fashion. While I may not practice all of them at a strictly professional level, I dive deep enough to achieve genuine excellence. This lifelong curiosity has made true versatility the core of my identity, allowing me to draw unique inspiration from multiple diverse fields rather than being confined to a single area of expertise.
